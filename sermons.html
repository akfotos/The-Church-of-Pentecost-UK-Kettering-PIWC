Study the Bible

A simple way to respond to every message

Use these four movements after listening to a sermon or reading a teaching outline.

01

Read

Return to the main passage and read it carefully in context.

02

Reflect

Ask what the passage reveals about God, people, and faithful living.

03

Respond

Choose one specific action, prayer, or change prompted by the Word.

04

Share

Discuss what you learned with another believer and encourage one another.

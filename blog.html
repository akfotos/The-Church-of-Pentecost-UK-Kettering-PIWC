Finding Strength in God’s Presence

Life can feel uncertain, but God’s presence remains constant. Prayer is not simply a response to difficulty; it is a daily invitation to know God, hear His voice, and receive strength for each step.

“God is our refuge and strength, an ever-present help in trouble.” Psalm 46:1
Read the full reflection

Strength in God begins with making room for Him. Set aside a few quiet minutes, open Scripture, and speak honestly about what is on your heart. You do not need perfect words. Begin where you are.

As you pray, remember God’s faithfulness. Write down answered prayers, promises from Scripture, and moments when He sustained you. Let remembrance replace fear with gratitude.

Finally, do not walk alone. Share your burdens with trusted believers who can pray with you. God often brings encouragement through the church community.

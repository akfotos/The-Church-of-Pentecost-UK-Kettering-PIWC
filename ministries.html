Our Ministries

Grow in Christ, build meaningful relationships, discover your gifts, and serve God alongside others.

More Than a Sunday

Faith lived together

Our ministries help every generation move from attending church to belonging, growing, and making a difference. Each group creates space for biblical teaching, prayer, fellowship, practical care, and service.

You do not need previous experience to get involved. Whether you are new to faith, new to Kettering PIWC, or ready to lead, we will help you find a suitable next step.

How ministries help you grow

  • Know God: Learn and apply Scripture.
  • Find community: Build honest, supportive relationships.
  • Discover purpose: Recognise and develop your gifts.
  • Serve others: Put faith into action in church and the community.

Use Your Gifts

Your next step can start today

You may already know where you want to serve, or you may need help discovering your strengths. Either way, begin with a conversation.

  1. Tell us about yourself.Share your interests, experience, availability, and the areas that matter to you.
  2. Meet a ministry leader.Learn about the team, expectations, training, and any required safeguarding steps.
  3. Try a suitable role.Serve alongside an experienced team member and receive support as you grow.

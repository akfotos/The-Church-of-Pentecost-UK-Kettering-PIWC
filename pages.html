Who We Are

About Our Church

A Christ-centred, Spirit-filled community where people from every background can worship, grow, serve, and belong.

Our Story

Called to make disciples and transform communities

The Church of Pentecost UK Kettering PIWC exists to share the good news of Jesus Christ and equip believers to live out their faith every day.

We are part of a global Pentecostal family committed to prayer, biblical teaching, fellowship, evangelism, and compassionate service.

Our Vision

To become a vibrant, Spirit-filled church that equips people to influence Kettering and beyond with the values of the Kingdom of God.

Our Mission

We worship God, proclaim the gospel, disciple believers, strengthen families, and serve our community with practical love.

Our Beliefs

We believe in the authority of Scripture, salvation through Jesus Christ, the work of the Holy Spirit, prayer, and the fellowship of believers.
